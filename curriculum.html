I’m a physicist and AI research scientist. My background is in computational neuroscience: during my doctoral and postdoctoral work at IIT, I studied community structure and spectral entropy in brain functional connectivity networks. I now work on language model interpretability and reasoning, alongside scientific software and portfolio optimization.

Based in Milan / Rovereto, Italy. Fellow of the Thinking About Thinking Society and board member at skfolio Labs.
