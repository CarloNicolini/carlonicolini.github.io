# Writing classification

Science is the parent field for AI, Physics, Math, Finance, and Neuroscience.
Philosophy is separate; Engineering keeps practical computing notes discoverable.
Cross-list only when a second subject is substantive. Statistical mechanics applied
to reasoning can be AI and Physics; a philosophical essay about artificial cognition
can be Philosophy and AI. A benchmark named “philosophy” is not automatically a
philosophical essay, and a software “second brain” is not neuroscience.

These assignments were reviewed from the titles and Markdown headings of all 185
articles, including drafts. Existing URLs are pinned with explicit permalinks.
Category counts overlap because an article can appear in more than one field.
