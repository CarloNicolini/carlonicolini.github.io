I’m developing a programme of individual lessons for newcomers who would like to understand the ideas behind AI and quantitative portfolio methods, and learn to work with them in Python.

The plans below are a starting point. We would agree on a goal, work out what you already know, and adjust the order and pace. You can follow a complete route or focus on a particular lesson.

How I propose to teach

Format
One-to-one, online
A lesson
60–90 minutes, adjusted to the topic
Between lessons
A small exercise to discuss next time

Each meeting combines an explanation, a worked example, and time for your questions.
