A nice update from the TMLR Editors-in-Chief: our paper, [*Hyperdimensional Probe: Decoding LLM Representations via Vector Symbolic Architectures*](https://openreview.net/forum?id=WxM7lIoGBb), recently received **J2C certification**. We are planning to present the work at **ICLR 2027** through the Journal-to-Conference track.

The [certification](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/editorial-policies.html#certifications) recognizes work suitable for presentation at a participating conference and opens that opportunity to TMLR papers. I am happy to see this recognition for our work, and grateful to my coauthors and the TMLR editors and reviewers.

The [paper](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=WxM7lIoGBb) and [code](https://github.com/Ipazia-AI/hyperprobe) are available online.
